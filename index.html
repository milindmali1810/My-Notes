Human in the loop

Telegram → LinkedIn draft pipeline

Jot a raw note in a Telegram channel. The pipeline filters it hard, and only if it's worth posting, drafts it in your voice with a fresh news angle and sends it back to you on Telegram for review.

Nothing is ever posted to LinkedIn automatically. There is no LinkedIn integration in the code, so there is nothing that could publish on its own.

How it works

  1. 1
    TriggerTelegram calls the webhook whenever a new post lands in the source channel.
  2. 2
    InputThe raw fragment is logged as-is with a timestamp. No cleanup.
  3. 3
    ContextReads your voice guide (skill.txt), finds the crux of the note, and pulls relevant recent news with sources.
  4. 4
    FilterA strict scoring pass. Most fragments fail, and that is the pipeline working as intended.
  5. 5
    DraftOnly fragments that pass get a LinkedIn draft, written in your voice around a news angle.
  6. 6
    Review & memoryThe draft (or the rejection, with sources) is DMed to you. Reply APPROVE or REJECT and the decision is stored in Supabase.

Built with

Telegram Bot APIChannel posts in, review messages out.
GeminiScoring, crux extraction and drafting.
SupabaseMemory of approved and rejected drafts.
Flask on VercelServerless webhook at /api/webhook.
